Topik Proyek: Analisis Model Ancaman (Threat Modeling) & Arsitektur Pertahanan Berlapis (Defense in Depth) pada Sistem Pembayaran Elektronik (FinTech E-Wallet).


Cakupan Vektor Serangan:

  • Vektor 1: Mobile Banking Trojan, Sideloading APK, Overlay Cloak & Dagger, Accessibility Service Hijacking (Sisi Client).
  • Vektor 2: Phishing & Adversary-in-the-Middle (AitM) Reverse Proxy (Evilginx) untuk Bypass 2FA SMS & Cookie Theft (Jalur Identitas).
  • Vektor 3A: Rogue Access Point, ARP Poisoning, SSL Stripping & Replay Attack (Jalur Jaringan).
  • Vektor 3B: API Broken Object Level Authorization (BOLA / IDOR) & OPA Rego Authorization (Infrastruktur API).
  • Vektor 3C: Layer 7 API Flooding / DDoS, Database Connection Pool Exhaustion, Anycast WAF & Circuit Breaker (Backend).
  • Ketahanan Data: Skema Pencadangan 3-2-1, Immutable WORM Storage, & DRC Failover (Resiliensi Ledger).
